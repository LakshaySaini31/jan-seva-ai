          Systemic UX & Architecture Audit
          
How Jan Seva AI Solves Citizens' Top 5 Frustrations with myScheme

        

        
      

      

        

          

            
 Old Portal Problem #1: 7-Page Static Wizard

            
Users must click through 7 separate screens (Gender → Age → State → Caste → Occupation → Income) just to see results. Changing 1 parameter forces restarting.

          

          

            
 Jan Seva AI Fix: Bhashini NLP + 1-Screen Live Sliders

            
Type a single sentence or slide Age/Income on the left sidebar to watch eligibility recalculate in 0ms without a single page reload.

          


          

            
 Old Portal Problem #2: Black-Box Eligibility & Legalese

            
Schemes simply disappear when you don't qualify, leaving citizens guessing if they missed by ₹5,000 income or 1% marks. Guidelines are copied verbatim from Gazette PDFs.

          

          

            
 Jan Seva AI Fix: Rule-by-Rule Pills & Plain-Language Toggle

            
Every card explicitly displays ✔ Age 18 fits 17-25 or ✖ Income ₹5L exceeds ₹2.5L cap, plus a 1-click toggle between AI Simple Summary and Gazette Clause.

          


          

            
 Old Portal Problem #3: Dead-End Redirects & Doc Rejections

            
"Apply Now" dumps users onto outdated external state portals where they discover halfway through that their Income Certificate or NPCI Bank Seeding is missing.

          

          

            
 Jan Seva AI Fix: Pre-Flight DigiLocker Vault & 1-Click Apply

            
Cross-checks your DigiLocker vault before you apply, highlights missing certificates with e-District turnaround times, and submits via a Unified Single Application API.
